I am an M.S. in FinTech student at the University of South Florida. My research interests center on financial markets, the incorporation of information into prices, and machine learning methods in finance.

My research experience includes financial text analysis and machine learning-based portfolio construction at Sungkyunkwan University, under the supervision of Prof. Daejin Kim. At USF, I pursue an independent study in empirical asset pricing replication supervised by Prof. Matthew G. Son.
