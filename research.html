Inquiry Disclosures, Corporate Responses, and Stock Price Delay: Generative AI-Based Text Analysis

Sungkyunkwan University · Advisor: Prof. Daejin Kim

This study examines how inquiry disclosure frequency and the informativeness of firms’ responses relate to stock price delay in the Korean stock market.

Research summary

A GPT-4.1-based measure evaluates the clarity and specificity of corporate responses to market rumors and news reports. Firm-year regression analyses examine the relationship between this measure and stock price delay. A formal abstract and manuscript will be added when available.
