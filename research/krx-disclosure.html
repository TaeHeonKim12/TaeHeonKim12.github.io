RESEARCH PROJECT

Inquiry Disclosures, Corporate Responses, and Stock Price Delay

Generative AI-Based Text Analysis

Sungkyunkwan University · Advisor: Prof. Daejin Kim

Manuscript status to be confirmed

Abstract

Formal abstract forthcoming. The summary below is based on the current academic CV.

This project examines the relationships between inquiry disclosure frequency, firms’ initial response characteristics, and stock price delay in the Korean stock market. It uses generative AI to measure the informativeness of corporate responses to market rumors and news reports.

Methodology

  • GPT-4.1-based evaluation of response clarity and specificity.
  • Construction of a measure of disclosure informativeness.
  • Firm-year regression analyses of disclosure characteristics and stock price delay.

Findings

The current CV reports an association between more informative responses and lower stock price delay. Detailed estimates, sample definitions, and robustness analyses are not included in this website yet.

This summary does not establish a causal effect.
